**Fewer clicks on the desktop, undo, and old scans cleaned up.**

- **Undo and redo** for notes, highlights and form fields: the arrows in the bar, or ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). Save only shows while the document differs from its file.
- **Wide windows (the Mac app, a desktop browser):** Find and Details stay open beside the pages, each closed and opened on its own (remembered); every action is a button in the bar, no ⋯ menu. In Documents every filter (tag, sender, type, year) is its own chip.
- **Clean up pages** (⋯ on a scanned document): a PDF from another scan app goes through Tessel’s scanner — crop, turn, filters — and is saved back into the same file, with its details and its text read again.
- **Details suggested by the model** for any document, not only new scans: **Suggest with AI** in Details fills in what is still empty; nothing is saved until you press Save.
- **Improve text with AI** is only offered where there are scanned pages to read again (not on typed documents).
