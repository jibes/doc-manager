**Camera: the whole picture.** The scanner asked the camera for a widescreen picture, which cuts the top and bottom off a phone's 4:3 sensor, and the preview showed only that part. It now uses the camera's own full size and shape: the preview shows everything the camera sees (with black bars where its shape differs from the screen's), and pages are found and taken from all of it.

**AI reading with more models.** A model that can't follow a JSON schema and says so only once its reply has started (DeepSeek: “does not support JSON schema mode”) is asked again for plain JSON, then without a format. The format that worked is used first next time.

**Make searchable for mixed documents.** A PDF whose first pages are typed and whose later pages are scans now offers ⋯ → Make searchable too: text is read and added for the pages that have none.
