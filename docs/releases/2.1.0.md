**All files access, off means off.** Turning off “Show every document on this device” now takes the device's other documents out of Documents and search at once, not only out of Folders. Android keeps the permission itself until you withdraw it in its settings.

**Sorting both ways.** The order beside the count names what a list is sorted by (last opened, date, name, size); tap it to choose another. The arrow beside it turns the order round: oldest or smallest first, Z–A. Saved views keep the direction, and views saved as “Oldest first” open as date, turned.

**Long lists load as you scroll.** Documents shows the first 100 and adds more as you near the end, instead of a Show more button.

**No Select button.** Long-press a row (right-click on a computer) to start picking; ✕ in the bar ends it. Folder lists sit a little lower under their count, which now fits on one line.

**100 % at the end.** Scrolled all the way down, a document reads 100 %, in the reader and on Reading, also where its last page is shorter than the screen and never became the current page. It stays so when opened again, and on your other devices.

**Open file is on Reading only.** Folders no longer repeats it.

**Holding longer still selects.** A long press that lasted more than about a second could let go of the item it had just picked. Also: Reading no longer has a link to all recently opened documents; on a phone, Documents opens in that order.
