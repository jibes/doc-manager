**A trash for the archive.** Deleting something in your archive folder now moves it to the archive's trash instead of deleting it for good, with Undo right there. Since the trash syncs too, you can still get back a document that was deleted on another device. Settings → Archive folder → Trash shows what's in it, to restore or delete for good; after 30 days items go by themselves.

**Sync conflicts and duplicates.** When two devices change the same document before syncing, Syncthing keeps both versions. Tessel now notices these (and documents that are in the archive twice), shows a note in the library, and lets you keep the right one, or both. The one you don't keep goes to the trash. The inbox also tells you when a new document is already filed.

**Safer saving.** In the Android app, files are now written in full before they replace the old version, so a sync never picks up a half-written file.
