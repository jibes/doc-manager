**Inbox.** Choose your synced folder as the archive (Settings → Archive folder) and its Inbox folder becomes the place new documents land: scans save there by default, and anything you or another device drops in shows up too. On one device, turn on Settings → Sort the inbox on this device. The inbox then shows each new document with details filled in for you (its own, a date found in it, and your rules) and where it will go, such as `2026/KoRo/2026-09-28 Lieferschein.pdf`. Check them, change what's wrong, and tap File: the details are written into the PDF and it moves into place.

**Rules.** In the inbox, a rule like "when the text has *KoRo*: sender KoRo, type Lieferschein, tag Lebensmittel" fills in matching documents. Rules and the folder template are kept in the archive, so every device has them. With AI reading set up, "Suggest with AI" fills in the rest from the document's text.
