**Faster scans, and white pages in Dark.**

- **Documents made searchable (or improved with AI) open much faster and are much smaller.** Tessel wrote its hidden text one word at a time, and each word got a font of its own in the file: a page of small print held thousands. The text is now written a line at a time — about ten times fewer objects. Documents done before this version stay as they are until they are read again (Improve text with AI, or Clean up pages, which also writes a fresh, compact file).
- **In Dark, a page’s white is the app’s own soft white**, not the dimmed tan that made every page look like a poor scan.
- **A scan’s greyish or yellowed paper is shown white** (in page view, thumbnails and the text view), as typed pages are. Only the display changes, not the file.
