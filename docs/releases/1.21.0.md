**Each document once on Reading.** Recently opened lists only documents not shown above it: the one to continue and those on the reading list are no longer repeated there. The Continue card and the reading list are picked like rows (long press or right click), to take documents off the list, share them or remove them.

**This device means Tessel's own storage.** In the Android app, files in the Tessel folder (scans, files opened from other apps) count as This device, as library copies do in a browser; Other folders are the folders you added. The place filter and each row's place follow.

**Books in Documents.** EPUBs in your folders and library are listed and found by title, author, date and subjects, with the type Book. Their text isn't searched yet.

**Folders inside folders.** A folder added inside another one no longer lists its files twice: the archive keeps everything below it, and elsewhere the inner folder lists its own files. Adding such a folder says where its files count.
