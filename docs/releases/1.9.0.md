**Search all your documents.** The search field in the library now also looks inside every PDF in your folders (and their subfolders) and in the library: their text, title, sender, tags and file name. A result shows the words around the match; tap it and the document opens at that page with the match highlighted. Tessel reads your documents for this in the background, on each device, and keeps the index on the device only. While you read, it waits.

**Details for every PDF** (new since 1.7): ⋯ → Details gives a PDF a title, sender, type, date and tags, saved in the PDF itself, so other apps and your other devices see them too. Saving only adds a few hundred bytes to the end of the file.
