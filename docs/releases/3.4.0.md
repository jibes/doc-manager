**Text is read in the background.** Make searchable and Improve text with AI no longer hold the document: they go on while you read it, look at another or leave the app. A scan read with AI is saved and opened at once with the text read on the device; the model reads it afterwards, fills in its details and, when you gave it no name, names it.

While a document waits or is being read, it says so at the top; once its text is in the file, it opens again with it. Unsaved notes or form entries are never written over: the text waits until they are saved. What is left to read when the app closes is read when it opens again. Everything added keeps its text read the same way, one document after another.

If the model can’t read a scan, it keeps the device’s text, and Documents says so.

**AI reading answers faster** (3.3.0, now also in the Android app). A new switch in Settings, **Let the model think**, is off by default: the model is asked to answer at once, which is much faster, and reading pages rarely needs more. There is no common way to ask for that, so Tessel asks in every way servers know (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Kimi, vLLM, Anthropic); a server that doesn’t know one says so, and Tessel asks again without it and remembers. A model that can’t follow Tessel’s answer format is remembered on the device too.
