**Tidy up: sort documents in by their content, and remove what stays in one tap.**

Step 2 of Tidy up now offers two ways:
- **By name** (as before): each other name is moved under a category. New: **Remove all** on "Stay as they are" removes every name in that section; **Keep all** on "Removed" brings them back.
- **By content**: the model reads the start of each document (about 2,000 characters, 5 documents per request) and gives it the categories that fit, chosen only from your list. They replace the document's current tags (or its type). Names of companies still move to Sender.
  - Before it starts, it says how many documents and requests that is, and where the text goes. It can be stopped and continued.
  - The list shows each document with old → new. Untick one to leave it as it is. Documents that already fit, or got no answer, stay unchanged.
  - By content is the default when Tidy up is opened for documents selected in the list.
