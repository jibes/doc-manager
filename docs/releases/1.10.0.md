**Documents.** A new screen (the documents button in the library, or "Filter" next to search results) lists every document in your library and folders. Narrow it by tag, sender, type and year: each choice shows how many documents it leaves. Sort it newest first or by name, and search it as before.

**Saved views.** Keep a search and its filters as a view ("Invoices 2026", "Insurance") and get back to it with one tap. Choose your synced folder in Settings → Archive folder and views are kept in it (in `.tessel/views.json`), so your other devices have them too.
