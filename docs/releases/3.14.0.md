**Tidy up tags, redone: you choose the categories, and everything else is sorted into them.**

Documents → ⋯ → Tidy up tags now works in three steps, separately for tags and for types:

1. **Choose categories.** All names in use are listed with how many documents have each and how general it is (●●●○○, rated by the AI). You can sort by most used or most general. The AI's suggestion of 10 to 15 categories comes ticked; tick others, untick some, or add a new one. A line shows how many documents already have one of them.
2. **Sort in the rest.** Every other name is listed under the category it goes into: the AI proposes one, and where none fits, the name stays as it is. Tap a name to move it to another category, keep it as its own tag, or remove it.
3. **Check and apply.** The names left after the change and how many documents have each. Then "Change N documents".

Along the way, unless switched off:
- A name written several ways becomes one.
- Tags that only repeat the type or the sender go.
- Tags that are names of companies or people (Zurich, Allianz, …) move to the Sender field where it is empty, and leave the tags.

Gone: crossed-out names (they looked like deletions but meant "unchanged") and the mixed list of three levels.
