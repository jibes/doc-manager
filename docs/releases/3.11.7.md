**A new scan never replaces another document's pages.**

- **Cleaning up pages left without saving** no longer turns the next new scan into a replacement for that document: Add → Camera asks whether to go on with them or start a new scan. Stopping a clean-up says the document stays as it is, and goes back to it.
- **Text view keeps your place** when the phone is turned or the window resized.
- **Smaller things:** the scanner's bottom bar stays above the filter chips; Esc leaves the crop screen; importing a folder again says how many documents are really new; the guide no longer mentions themes the app doesn't have.
