**One home for your documents.** Tessel now has two places instead of five. The **Tessel folder** is everything Tessel keeps: scans, files you keep, the inbox and the trash. Choose the folder you sync and all of it reaches your other devices; without one, it is Documents/Tessel on Android or the browser's own storage. **Sources** are the other folders Tessel reads: folders you add, and the whole device with all files access, browsed by its folders. Documents filters by these two, and Folders lists the Tessel folder first, then the sources. Saved views that filtered by the old places keep working.

**Open to look, keep to save.** A file opened from another app, a share, Open file or a drop is now only viewed: nothing is copied and it isn't listed. Tessel remembers where you left the last 20 such files on this device, so opening one again picks up there (Settings can forget them). The first time you save something about it (a bookmark, the reading list, a form, notes, details, Make searchable, Improve text with AI) Tessel asks to keep it: a file picked in place stays where it is; anything else is copied into the Tessel folder (its Inbox, when you have chosen one). ⋯ → Keep in Tessel does it at once.

**Reading is your reading list.** Reading shows the reading list, with Continue first. Without one, it shows the last three documents you opened and a link to all of them: Documents, sorted by **Opened recently**, which is also the order Documents opens in on phones.

**The guide** that comes with Tessel describes the new way of opening files.
