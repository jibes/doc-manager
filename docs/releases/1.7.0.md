**Better text in scans.** Words in a scan's text no longer run together when you copy or search them ("Zahlbar bis", not "Zahlbarbis"). Pages with columns, like a payment slip, are read column by column, while forms and tables are still read across.

**Read scans with AI (optional).** In Settings → AI reading, enter the address, key and model of an AI service: Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible one (OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio, …). Then a scan offers "Read with AI": it fixes the recognised text, reads handwriting, and names the file by date, sender and title ("2026-10-02 Caritas Zürich – Rechnung 61106"), with sender, type and keywords in the PDF. The pages are only sent when you switch it on for a scan, and the key stays on your device. If the service can't be reached, the scan is saved with the text read on the device.
