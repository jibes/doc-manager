**Nothing you do gets lost.** A scan that can't be kept stays to try again, with any pages added while it was saving. Details and Keep in archive keep your saved highlights and notes. The Android back gesture and the browser's Back ask before leaving unsaved edits. Deleting a note twice in a row no longer deletes the next annotation. Saved views, rules and the trash list are never overwritten when the archive can't be read yet; Tessel says so instead.

**A safer trash and Sync.** Items go for good only 30 days after they were trashed, never by the file's own date, and Restore puts them back in their folder under their own name, on any device. Delete for good asks first. On the Sync screen, Keep this asks before deleting the other version for good (outside the archive folder), and Keep both says what it did.

**Search finds everything.** Every match counts: the number, the filters, the order and Show more cover all of them, and every row shows the words around its match. Matches start at the start of a word: "Bahn" shows "Deutsche Bahn", not "Autobahn". Documents follows as files are added, moved, renamed or deleted, without flashing "No documents" on the way.

**The archive keeps up.** A locked archive stays searchable, and moved or renamed documents aren't read again. Filed documents are recognised when they come again, and Inbox copies aren't counted as duplicates. Filing keeps the library's reading position and bookmarks, and the Inbox count follows. Rules match whole words (ß matches SS), two-digit years and birthdays no longer file letters under 2067, and a template without a type leaves no " - - ". Removing a reading-list document from Tessel keeps it off the list on your other devices.

**Documents and Folders.** Saved views keep the place and order. Undo belongs to its message. Search and filters stay when you switch to Folders and back. Renaming a document keeps its .pdf. Files dropped anywhere open. All lists have the same width, and German labels fit.

**Scanner and AI reading.** Read with AI is switched on for one scan at a time. The AI address must start with https:// (or http://), so the key never goes anywhere else, and a reply cut off at the length limit says so. The camera stops under the crop screen, a deleted page can be undone, and pages saved without text are reported.

**Android.** Printing and downloading large PDFs no longer freeze the app. Cancelling AI reading stops the upload. AI models on your network (Ollama, LM Studio) work over http.

**Web.** Each release brings its own offline cache, so an update never runs with the previous PDF engine; the text recognition models stay downloaded.
