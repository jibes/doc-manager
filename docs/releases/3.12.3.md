**The date in the details (Angaben) is found.**

- The AI's date is kept, however the model writes it. Before, a date given as "02.10.2026" or with a time was thrown away, and the field stayed empty.
- With no date from the model, the first date in the document's text is taken, as the inbox already does.
- Without AI: when a document has no date of its own, Details offers the first date in its text ("Found in the document: 2026-10-03. Use it").
- More ways of writing a date are recognised: "3.Oktober 2026", "3.Okt.2026", "03. 10. 2026", "03/10/2026" (read day first) and "2026-10-3".
- A date can be typed as it is written on paper ("2.10.2026", "2. Oktober 2026"): it is saved as 2026-10-02. This works in Details, in Details of several documents, and in the inbox.
