**Pick up where you left off, on any device.** With an archive folder chosen, your reading list, where you are in each document and your bookmarks now follow you between devices that sync it. Start a document on the phone and it's on your desktop's reading list, open at the same place. Each device keeps its own small file in the archive's `.tessel` folder, so syncing never conflicts. Turn it off in Settings → Archive folder → Share reading with my other devices.
