**Searchable scans.** PDFs that are only pictures (scans from another app or a copier) can now be searched. Open one and choose ⋯ → Make searchable: Tessel reads the pages on your device and adds their text to the file, invisible, so the document looks the same but its words can be found, selected and copied. Documents filed from the inbox get this automatically, and the inbox's rules and dates now work on them too.

**More of the camera picture.** The scanner shows the camera's whole picture instead of filling the screen with a cropped part of it, so what you see is what is taken.

**AI reading in the app.** Reading a scan with AI could fail in the Android app with "Failed to fetch" after a long wait, while Test worked. Long readings now stream their answer, so they aren't cut off on the way, and the app makes the request itself. Should it still fail, the message now says what the server answered.

**Cleaner details.** Placeholder details that some programs write ("untitled", "anonymous") are ignored, so they no longer turn up as a document's title, sender or type.
