**One button per kind of action.** The reader's bar is now Find · Text view · Aa · ⋯.

**Find** brings search and contents together: a search field above the contents, pages, bookmarks and notes. Start typing and the list turns into matches; clear the field and the contents come back. ⌘F (Ctrl+F) opens it ready to type, T opens it on the contents.

**Text view** has its own button again, on phones too. It lights up while you're reading the text, and your place carries over both ways. Aa now only holds how the page or text looks.

In books, Reading list moved into ⋯, as in PDFs.
