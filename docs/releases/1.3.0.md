**Scan documents.** The new Scan button opens the camera: Tessel finds the page, takes it by itself once you hold still, and straightens it. Add more pages, choose a filter (Document, B&W, Grey, Colour, Original), turn, crop or retake pages, then save a searchable PDF into one of your folders or the library. Text recognition (PaddleOCR) and page detection run on the device; nothing is uploaded. Photos can be imported instead of taken.

**Library and Files are tabs.** On phones they sit along the bottom with Scan in the middle; on wider windows they are tabs in the header. Files has “Other location…” for single files from anywhere.

**Your documents are real files.** Scans and documents opened from other apps (mail, chat, downloads) are now kept in **Documents/Tessel** instead of hidden storage inside the app: other apps see them, backups include them, and they survive uninstalling. Copies kept by earlier versions move there on first start. Tessel appears first in Files. (Android 8–10 ask for storage access once.)
