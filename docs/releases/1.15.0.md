**A clearer Tessel: Reading, Scan, Documents.** The app is now organised around what you do:

- **Reading** (left): continue where you were, your reading list, the last documents you opened, and Open file.
- **Scan** (middle): make a new document.
- **Documents** (right): find and manage everything. One search field for the text and details of every document, filters by tag, sender, type, year and place, your saved views, and the Inbox and any sync issues right at the top (the Documents tab shows how many documents wait in the Inbox). **Folders** next to All is what used to be the Files tab: your archive first, marked as synced, with its Trash under it, then your other folders.

On a computer, the places are in a sidebar. The app opens where you left it.

**Keep in archive.** A document you opened from elsewhere lives only on this device. ⋯ → Keep in archive puts it in your archive's Inbox, so it syncs to your other devices and gets sorted, and keeps your place, reading list and bookmarks.
