**Faster, and never stuck.** Tessel no longer stalls after starting or when you open a document from another app. Search now builds and keeps its index in the background, away from the screen: the first search after this update takes a little while to get ready (once), later ones answer right away. After updating, the documents in your archive are read once more in the background (it pauses while you read).

**Sort by.** Next to the number of documents: newest or oldest first, name, or largest first (search results come best match first until you choose another order). Each document shows its date and the folders it is in.

**Sync issues you can read.** Each copy shows the folders it is in, its date and size, with Open and Keep this beside or under it. Only copies inside your archive count as duplicates; the same file elsewhere on your phone is not the archive's to tidy.
