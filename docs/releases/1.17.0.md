**Pick several documents at once.** Long press a document (right click on a computer), or tap Select, then tap more. The same actions everywhere, where they apply: Share, Reading list, Copy, Move, Rename, Delete. In Documents, Copy and Move take you to Folders to open the folder they go to; deleting in the archive goes to its trash, with Undo. In Reading: on or off the reading list, share, remove from the library.

**One look for every list of documents.** Reading, Documents and folders show documents the same way: as rows with a small first page beside the title, or as a grid of big first pages. The button beside Select switches every list at once. Documents now show their first pages too, and folders sort with the same Sort by menu (name, newest, oldest, largest).

**Sync issues resolve at once.** A conflict or duplicate you resolved leaves the list straight away and stays gone; a conflict copy in the archive is no longer listed twice when Tessel can see every document on the device.
