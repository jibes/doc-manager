**Tidy up by content: every document gets its answer.**

- Fixed: with a model that isn't held to a JSON schema (for example DeepSeek), most answers were dropped. Such a model may write a document's id as `3` or `"Document 3"` instead of `"3"`, list its entries without the outer object, or give the categories as one string. All of these are now understood.
- Documents the model leaves out of its answer are asked for once more.
- A document for which none of the categories fits is no longer sent again on "Go on". It is counted under the list: "For N documents none of the categories fits".
- The log notes, for each request, how many documents were asked for and how many were answered.
