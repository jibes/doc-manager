**Light, Dark or Auto.** Six themes become three. Auto follows your system, or the light around you in the Android app. In Dark, PDF pages keep their printed colours, dimmed and slightly warm, so a white page no longer glares at night; text view follows the appearance. Earlier theme choices carry over.

**Invert colours, per document.** In Aa (page view), for the PDFs you prefer as light text on dark. Tessel remembers it for that document.

**Tidier reader and library.** On phones, the reader's bar keeps Search, Contents and Aa, so long titles have room; Page/Text view is in Aa, and Reading list, Share, Download and Print are under ⋯. Long titles in Recent wrap to two lines, with actions behind ⋯ or a long press. Files shows names without .pdf and wraps long names cleanly. Removing a document and leaving unsaved changes ask in Tessel's own dialog.
