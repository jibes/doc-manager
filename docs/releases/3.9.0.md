**The text view reads letters as text and shows forms as they are.**

- **Forms, invoices and tables** stay as drawn: rows of short cells or amounts, with the labels between them, are shown as that part of the page instead of being flattened into scrambled lines. A page that is mostly that (an invoice, a prescription form) is shown whole, fitted to the screen; tap it to open the page and zoom.
- **Scanned pages:** their recognised text says little about size and weight, so headings now come from the height of the lines, and noise no longer turns ordinary lines into headings. A scanned form is shown as its picture, with ticked boxes, handwriting, stamps and signatures, which its text can’t hold.
- **Letters read better:** an address, a signature or a list of enclosures keeps its line breaks; “9. Dezember 2024” is a date, not a list item; a line the recogniser cut in two is one line again.
- **Fixed — Improve text with AI could add a line twice:** a model sometimes “adds” a table row the page already has, in the wrong place. Such text is no longer written into the file. (A page improved before this version may still hold such copies; they don’t change how it looks, only its hidden text.)

**Tessel on a big screen: fewer clicks, and a real Mac app** (3.8.0 wasn’t published; its changes come with this version):

- **List and document side by side** (desktop windows 1300 px and wider): a document opens beside the list, with its details; the list stays where it was.
- **The keyboard:** ↑ ↓ go through the list (beside it, each one opens), Enter opens, **Space** shows the first page without opening it, ⌘⌫ deletes, **⌘K** goes to any document, tag, sender or action, ⌘N scans, ⌘O opens a file.
- **Right-click a document** (with a mouse): open, open in a new window, quick look, details, share, rename, select, delete. A long press on a phone still picks.
- **Drag and drop:** drag a document out of the list to the Finder or an email; drop documents on the **Tag** chip to tag them.
- **Details of several documents at once:** pick them, then **Details** — set the sender, type or date of all, add or remove tags; what you don’t touch stays as each one has it.
- **Wide windows** show the list’s Inbox, Trash, Rules … as buttons of their own instead of a ⋯ menu.
- **The Mac app:** a menu bar (File, Edit, View, Window, Help) with the shortcuts; documents open in windows of their own; the window opens where you left it; it follows the system’s dark mode from the first frame; it says when a new version is out (Help → Check for Updates…).
- AI actions have their own sparkles icon.
- Fixed: after **Clean up pages** the list kept showing the old picture of the document.
